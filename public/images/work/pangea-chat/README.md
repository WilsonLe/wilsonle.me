# Pangea Chat app captures

These images were captured from `https://app.pangea.chat/` on 12 November 2026
for Wilson's requested portfolio showcase. They show a task-created example
conversation with Pangea Bot, not private conversations with other people.
The app's artwork and interface belong to Pangea Chat.

- `conversation.webp`: Spanish role-play conversation and in-chat vocabulary.
- `discover.webp`: the world map and an activity card.
- `roleplay.webp`: an activity offers bot or peer participation.
- `translation.webp`: interactive translation choices in the message composer.
- `vocabulary.webp`: meaning and pronunciation for “monopatín”.

The original JPEG captures were converted to WebP with Sharp at quality 88,
preserving all image content and their 626 × 763 dimensions. Current screenshots
illustrate the product; they do not attribute every current feature to Wilson.
Contribution copy is based on the portfolio's existing public statement and its
historical source at commit `f557270d8c3aaebd63ab688039588845ca5becef`.
